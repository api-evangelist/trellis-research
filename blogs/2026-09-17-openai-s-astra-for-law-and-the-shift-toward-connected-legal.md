OpenAI has introduced Astra for Law, utilizing GPT-6 Astra to enhance legal research by integrating a Legal Search Index and 26 plugins, including Trellis for accessing state trial court data. This innovation helps attorneys gain insights into litigation patterns, judge rulings, and opposing counsel strategies, transforming legal research into a connected, multifaceted process.
